📖 Walkthrough Guide

Step 1: Reserve Gate 24 First

Set aside 4 items that share no common shape OR colour (e.g., Red Circle, Blue Triangle, Green Square, Yellow Star).

Step 2: Reserve Gate 12

Pick 3 items of the exact same shape in 3 different colours (e.g., Red Hexagon, Blue Hexagon, Green Hexagon).

Step 3: Reserve Gate 18 & 8

Allocate 4 items covering Red, Blue, Green, Yellow for Gate 18, and 3 distinct shapes for Gate 8.

Spawn Point (Room 7)

Begin at bottom-center Room 7. Traverse through open doors into unsealed rooms to gather all items before placing them on gate podiums.

Central Spine Ascent

Advance north through the central maze corridor: 7 ➔ 2 ➔ 13 ➔ 21 ➔ 12 ➔ 24.
